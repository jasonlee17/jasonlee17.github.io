Hello! I am Jason (Jaemyoung) Lee, a Postdoctoral Research Associate at the University of Wisconsin–Madison.

I work on Type Ia supernova (SN Ia) cosmology and baryon acoustic oscillations (BAO). I am deeply involved in the Vera C. Rubin Observatory Legacy Survey of Space and Time Dark Energy Science Collaboration (LSST DESC), particularly through the Time Domain and Pixels-to-Light-Curves working groups. I was also a key member of the Dark Energy Survey Supernova Working Group.

My research focuses on using astronomical observations to understand the expansion history of the universe, including wavelength-dependent atmospheric effects in supernova measurements and robust cosmological distance scales derived from large-scale structure.
